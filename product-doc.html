01 · TL;DR

Strike Zone: Ducks at Bat is a fast, two-round casino original for Vegas Infinite. The player is a Squeaky Slugger (VI Ducky in baseball gear) facing a Blubo pitcher at a full count. In Round 1 they place bets on a 3×3 strike-zone grid and watch a first-person pitch cinematic resolve to a hit (in-zone) or strikeout (out-of-zone, the house edge). On contact, Round 2 asks them to pick a swing — Contact, Power, or Grand Slam — each with its own risk curve, the last feeding the always-visible Progressive Jackpot, fed by 1% of every wager and won outright on a Grand-Slam HR.

Single session is ~30 seconds, ~$0.30–$30 per game. Designed for portrait and landscape web, deployed on Vercel.

Round 1

Bet the zone

9-cell strike zone. Single bets pay 9×, line bets pay 3×. 4% of pitches miss the zone for a strikeout — the house edge.

Round 2

Pick a swing

Three risk curves: Contact (1.25× safe), Power (2.0× mid), or Grand Slam (wins the progressive pool, rare). Opt 1 & 2 are tuned to 95% RTP. Opt 3 has lower deterministic RTP (~46%) but is the only path to win the progressive pool — a separate side bonus, not counted in the headline RTP.

Jackpot

Progressive pool

Always shown at top-centre. Grows by 1% of every wager. Won outright on Option 3 (Grand Slam) at 0.5% per attempt.

02 · Concept & Story

The matchup

Team Lucky Logic (defence) is the Blubos — translucent blue cube creatures with pixel-grin faces, wearing black jerseys and caps stamped with the Lucky Logic green-cube logo. They pitch, catch, and field.

Team VI (offence) is led by the Vegas Infinite Ducky in a black puffer vest and batting helmet stamped with the VI spade-and-infinity logo, plus pinstripe pants. Yellow rubber, gold beak, all swagger. Every game session, the Ducky steps into the batter's box for Team VI.

Brand-bible note: the VI Brief excludes Meta Avatars. Blubo characters must be re-designed as VI-original blue cube/robot characters (we keep the silhouette / vest motif, redraw the face and body so they are not Meta IP).

The count

Every game opens at 2 strikes, 3 balls — a full count. One pitch decides everything. This framing gives the player constant high-stakes tension and naturally explains the one-pitch-per-game structure: it's not artificial pacing, it's baseball.

Why it fits VI

  • Original. Strike-zone betting on a 3×3 grid married to a baseball-themed two-round risk ladder doesn't exist on a casino floor.
  • Branded. Uses the VI Ducky mascot natively. Renders in VI's neon-on-flood-black palette with Capitana type and hex holding devices.
  • Web-ready. Short sessions, low spec, video overlays drive emotion. No physics. Tappable on phone, beautiful on desktop.
  • Inclusive. Baseball is universal; the grid is legible; the maths is transparent. Single click to play.

03 · Game Flow

High-level player journey. Each node is a screen the player sees.

04 · Round 1 — Strike Zone

The 3×3 strike zone is rendered as a neon-edged grid floating over the behind-the-plate gameplay scene. The player first picks an active chip from the 4-chip rack ($1 / $5 / $25 / $100), then taps cells to place zone bets or row/column/diagonal markers to place line bets. Multiple simultaneous bets are allowed; switching the active chip reprices every existing bet in real time.

Bet typeCoverageStakePaysWin condition
Zone (single cell) 1 of 9 cells 1× active chip 9× stake Ball lands in that exact cell
Line 3 cells in a row, column, or diagonal (8 lines total) 1× active chip 3× stake Ball lands in any of the 3 cells

Pitch outcome distribution

The pitch result is computed at the moment the player taps Throw pitch. The video plays, then the resolve frame highlights the result cell (or an out-of-zone position).

OutcomeProbabilityEffect
Outside zone5.0%Strikeout. All R1 bets lost. Game over.
In zone (any cell)95.0%Contact. Pay matching R1 bets. Advance to R2.
· per individual cell10.556%Uniform across the 9 cells (95% / 9).

RTP math

Zone bet EV = (0.95 / 9) × 9 + (1 − 0.95 / 9) × 0 = 0.95
Line bet EV = (0.95 × 3 / 9) × 3 = 0.95

Both bet types share an identical 95% RTP — this is the headline player return. The 5% house edge is split: 4% true house take + 1% diverted to the progressive jackpot pool. The pool is a separate side mechanic: it grows on every wager and pays out on a Grand-Slam HR (see §05), but pool wins are a bonus on top of the published RTP — not a guaranteed return — so we do not count them toward the headline figure.

Chip-based bet sizing

The active chip is the unit stake for every bet on the table. A zone bet stakes 1 × chip and pays 9 × stake; a line bet stakes 1 × chip and pays 3 × stake on a covered cell. Switching the active chip re-prices every existing bet in real time.

Active chip1 zone bet1 line bet1 zone + 1 line (total staked)
$1$1$1$2
$5$5$5$10
$25$25$25$50
$100$100$100$200

Worked example: with the $5 chip selected, 1 zone + 1 line = $5 + $5 = $10 staked. Switching the active chip to $100 immediately reprices the same bets to $100 + $100 = $200 (a 20× scale-up). The balance check fires on every chip switch as well as on every placement: if the reprice would exceed the player's balance, the switch is blocked and a tooltip prompts the player to remove bets first.

Edge cases

  • Multiple bets, same cell hit: if a zone + line both cover the winning cell, both pay independently.
  • Max bet: per-cell stake cap (configurable, default = 5× table minimum to keep jackpot exposure bounded).
  • Insufficient balance — placement: a zone/line tap is rejected (with a tooltip) if it would push total staked above balance.
  • Insufficient balance — chip up-switch: tapping a higher-denomination chip is blocked if the resulting reprice would exceed the player's balance; the player must remove bets first.
  • No bets placed: "Throw pitch" disabled; tooltip prompts a minimum stake.

05 · Round 2 — Swing Power

Round 2 only runs if Round 1 made contact. The player picks one of three swing options on three neon hex cards. The R2 stake equals the sum of all R1 bet stakes the player committed on this hand. Options 1 and 2 each independently target 95% RTP. Option 3 is the lottery option: its deterministic (non-HR) RTP is intentionally low (~46%), and the upside lives entirely in the rare HR roll, which pays the live progressive pool. Pool wins are a side bonus — not counted toward the published 95% headline RTP.

Option 1 — Contact swing Low risk

"Just put it in play." Usually a single, occasionally an out.

OutcomeProbabilityReturnContribution
Single76.0%1.25× stake0.950
Out (caught / grounded)24.0%0×0.000
RTP = 0.76 × 1.25 + 0.24 × 0 = 0.95

Option 2 — Power swing Mid risk

"Go for extra bases." Doubles are the main prize; singles are a push refund.

OutcomeProbabilityReturnContribution
Double40.0%2.0×0.800
Single (push refund)15.0%1.0×0.150
Out45.0%0×0.000
RTP = 0.40 × 2.0 + 0.15 × 1.0 + 0.45 × 0 = 0.95

Option 3 — Grand-slam swing High risk · Jackpot

"Swing for the fences." Mostly an out. Sometimes a triple. Occasionally a double. Once in 200, the player wins the entire progressive jackpot pool outright.

OutcomeProbabilityReturnNotes
🏆 Home Run (Jackpot)0.5%Progressive poolPlayer wins the full live pool; pool resets to its $1,000 seed.
Triple10.0%3× stakeContribution to immediate RTP: 0.300
Double8.0%2× stakeContribution to immediate RTP: 0.160
Out (fly-out / grounder)81.5%0×Contribution: 0.000
Deterministic (non-HR) RTP = 0.10 × 3 + 0.08 × 2 + 0.815 × 0 = 0.46

Opt 3 is the lottery option — most swings return nothing, and the 46% deterministic RTP is well below the 95% headline. The HR roll (0.5%) pays the entire live progressive pool, which is funded by 1% of every R1 wager. A pool win is treated as a side bonus, not part of the published RTP: lucky players hit huge windfalls, the long-run expected pool win value is reflected in the 1% pool-diversion line item, but no single Opt 3 attempt is "guaranteed" the pool.

Jackpot display

The Jackpot label is always visible at top centre. It shows the live progressive pool value in cream tabular numerals (e.g. JACKPOT $1,247). The pool grows by 1% of every committed R1 wager; the label ticks up with a brief cyan glow each time a pitch is committed. When the player is in Round 2 with Option 3 selected, the label pulses cyan/magenta to telegraph "this swing can win the whole pool".

The pool seeds at $1,000 on first install and persists across browser sessions. On a Grand-Slam HR, the player is credited the full pool value and the pool resets to the $1,000 seed.

06 · Wireframes

Lo-fi mockups. Boxes = layout placeholders. Annotations call out behaviour and copy.

W1 · Splash / Title

Logo, "TAP TO PLAY", balance bottom-left, settings bottom-right. Jackpot ticker on top bar shows the live progressive jackpot pool (persists across sessions; ticks up after each pitch commits).

W2 · R1 Bet placement (behind the plate)

4-chip rack at the bottom ($1 / $5 / $25 / $100); the active chip has a cyan ring. Tap a cell for a zone bet (1× chip) or tap one of the dotted line markers around the grid edge for a line bet (3× chip, one per covered cell). Tapping a different chip reprices every existing bet — blocked if the reprice would exceed balance. Top bar shows the live progressive jackpot plus balance/total-staked readout. Throw Pitch CTA stays disabled when staked > balance or staked = 0.

W3 · R1 Pitch video (16:9 overlay)

Full-bleed 16:9 video region. Blubo pitcher winds up, releases, ball arrives at camera. HUD elements fade out at video start, fade back in on resolve.

W4a · R1 Resolve — Contact

Contact key frame. Shows IMG-05 (swing and a hit) — ball glows on the winning cell, toast shows R1 winnings, Continue → R2.

W4b · R1 Resolve — Strikeout

Strikeout key frame. Shows IMG-04 (swing and a miss) — ball lit outside the grid, STRIKEOUT stamp, Play again → Splash.

W5 · R2 Swing select (three hex cards)

Three hex "holding device" cards — Contact (cyan), Power (magenta), Grand Slam (purple). Contact and Power show headline odds and the literal $ payout for the player's stake; Grand Slam shows the live progressive pool value (it pays the whole pool on HR, not a fixed multiplier). Swing CTA disabled until selection.

W6 · R2 Outcome (Win or Out)

Outcome label uses one of: SINGLE / DOUBLE / TRIPLE / HOME RUN! / OUT. All five variants share IMG-05 (swing and a hit) as the background — including OUT, since the bat did make contact and the result was simply fielded. The outcome is conveyed entirely by the overlay stamp, jackpot ticker, audio sting, and confetti particle burst. On HR the player wins the entire live pool; total-this-hand sums R1 + R2 wins. Play again resets bets and returns to W2 (R1 bet placement), keeping balance and the (reseeded) jackpot pool.
